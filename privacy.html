Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Free Chatbot ("the app", "we") is a small, independently run chat app. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored in hashed form by the authentication service, never in plain text).
  • Chat content: the messages you send, the AI's replies, and any custom "skills" (instructions) you create.
  • Settings: preferences such as theme, language, accent colour and text size.
  • Voice input: if you use the microphone button, speech recognition is performed by your browser or device, which may send audio to its provider under that provider's own policy. The app receives only the resulting text.

2. How chats are stored and who can see them

Chats are saved to your account so you can return to them on any device. The app's administrator can read stored chats (for example to maintain, debug and moderate the service). Please do not share passwords, financial details, health information or other sensitive data in chats.

3. How your messages are processed

To generate replies, your messages (and the conversation context) are sent to third-party AI model providers. If you turn on the web search tool, your query may also be sent to a search provider. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies, and we do not control how they handle it.

4. Local storage

The app uses your browser's local storage and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data.

5. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and account and chat data are stored with Supabase. These providers may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and chats until you delete them or ask us to. You can delete individual chats in the app. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords, but no online service is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
